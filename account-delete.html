Local Ledger Account Deletion

Welcome to the Local Ledger account deletion process. We understand that you may wish to remove your account and associated data. This page explains how to request permanent deletion of your Local Ledger account.

You are about to permanently delete your Local Ledger account and all associated data stored within Local Ledger. Once the deletion request is completed, some or all related information may no longer be recoverable, except where retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational reasons.

Processing time: The account deletion process may take up to 7 days to complete. If we need any additional information to verify or process your request, we will contact you directly using the information associated with your account.

Delete your account from inside the app

  1. Access the Menu: Open Local Ledger and tap the profile or menu icon, usually located in the top-right corner of the screen.
  2. Open your profile: Select Your Profile, Account Settings, or a similar option.
  3. Find account deletion: Scroll down inside your profile or account settings and tap the Delete My Account button.
  4. Confirm deletion: Review the warning message carefully, then tap Delete Your Account to continue.
